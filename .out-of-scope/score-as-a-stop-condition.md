# A score out of ten as the reason to stop

Decided 2026-11-16.

## What was proposed

An external prompt pattern: "keep taking screenshots until every element
scores a 9, otherwise do not stop your work loop".

Nothing anchors the scale, so the number comes out of how finished the work
feels at that moment. The model that scores is the model that building, so it
climbs toward the target as the pass gets tiring. It is a self-certifying
exit.

## What to do instead

Stop on the list instead. `ui-design` stage 9 ends a pass when a fix turns up
nothing new and the Floor list of `gates.md` is walked and clear.
