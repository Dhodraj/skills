# A borrowed rule that restates a rule we already have

Decided 2026-11-17.

## What was proposed

Four items from `emilkowalski/skills`. A frequency table deciding whether a
thing may animate. A rule for the absent value. A one-character name bullet.
A worst-case toggle paragraph.

Each restates something already written. `craft.md` already stops an entrance
on a repeated interaction and names a keystroke. It already gives a missing
value an en dash in the faint ink. It already separates visible size from hit
area. `journey-and-states.md` already demands real fixtures. The table's own
thresholds, 100+ and fifty times a day, are measured nowhere.

## What to do instead

Widen the rule that exists rather than adding a second one beside it. The
entrances paragraph in `craft.md` now covers every kind of motion, not only
an entrance, at a cost of three lines.
