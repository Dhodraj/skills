# A number out of 100 at the top of a review

Decided 2026-10-27.

## What was proposed

Opening a review with an overall score.

A number invites arguing about the number instead of fixing the blocker, and
the severity list already carries the order. Where the scorer is also the
builder, the number certifies itself.

## What to do instead

Sort findings by harm to the customer, and record what is sound as well as
what is broken. It is in `ui-review/SKILL.md`, section 10.
