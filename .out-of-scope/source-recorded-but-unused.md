# Recording a source and changing no rules

Decided 2026-11-13.

## What was proposed

Adding a source to `NOTICE` and `sources.json`, reading it, and taking
nothing from it.

A record with no change behind it suggests the rules were reviewed against
the source when they were not. It makes the attribution list longer and the
skills no better.

## What to do instead

Take the measurable rules that hold whatever the project looks like, or
reject the source and say why here.
